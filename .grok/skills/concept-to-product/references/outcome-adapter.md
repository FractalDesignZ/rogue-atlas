# Outcome adapter

Mapping data to **known / perceived / speculative** ranges is the future
adapter. It is how a human enjoys a theorem without being lied to.

## The three ranges

| Range | Topological name | Human name | Product move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Known** | Interior (□) | Already in the open set | "Stay. This is necessary if the story holds." |
| **Perceived** | Continuous geodesic | Reachable without a jump | "Walk. The adapter's reading, not a new law." |
| **Speculative** | Possibility without necessity (◊ \ □) | A jump | "Jump. Fluent, possible, not entailed." |

A jump is the CST name for a hallucination. Draw it as a cut, not as a
glowing insight.

## What humans actually use

Ship these, in this order of joy:

1. **Now scrubber** — they stand somewhere.
2. **From-here horizon** — next kind, next title, until jump.
3. **Stay / jump fork** with V = D_KL − C. Refuse V < 0.
4. **Outcome tape** — a filmstrip, not a table.
5. **Spoken / copied reading** — one paragraph, claim-class honest.
6. **Pin / compare / transfer** — hold a field, see what holds, warps, breaks.
7. **Card / pack** — PNG + JSON they can show someone.

If you add AI narration, it may only **restate** the ledger and the fork.
It may not introduce a fourth range called "I notice."

## Sequences worth playing

Not a timeline. Three motifs:

- **Foundation → expansion** — a prime seeds later growth.
- **Constraint → elimination** — a jump removes a barrier.
- **Dependency inversion** — a later node outvalues its founding prime.

Play them as walks on the existing field. Do not spawn a second visualization
language.

## Compass scenes

Short, named postures beat generic empty states:

- Keep the room.
- Let it surprise.
- Don't fall.
- Follow the load-bearing.

Each scene is a preset of lens + fork + geodesic overlay. Fun is the
posture. The math does not change.

## Modality lens

□ necessary / ◊ possible / unentailed. Dimming the field by lens is
allowed. Inventing a new color for "the model is excited" is not.
