# Rogue Atlas — the synthesis

The platform is not a zeta visualizer plus a drilling dashboard. It is an
evidence-aware geometric intelligence studio:

> Transform sequential data into geometric and topological representations,
> while continuously distinguishing structure imposed by the representation
> from structure independently supported by the data.

## Three rooms

- **Atlas Research** — `/studio`. The zeta manuscript is the reference demo.
- **Atlas Operations** — `/adapter`. GT-MoE Rogue is the first vertical.
- **Atlas Audit** — `/atlas`. Evidence statuses, ladder, coordinate roles.

## Constitution (do not drift)

1. Time is typed. Spectral ordinate ≠ physical timestamp ≠ delay window ≠ 3D view.
2. Two graphs. Local is not recurrence.
3. δ² = 0 is the sheaf gate. Function restriction is an identity.
4. Six evidence statuses: exact, certified, observed, validated, failed, speculative.
5. Baseline-first ladder. Topology does not promote without a holdout. MoE does not promote until a specialist beats Level 1.
6. GUE is a spacing null. Not a drill null. Not a thought null.
7. Betti signatures are candidates, not identities.
8. Geometry does not certify safety. Interlocks stay deterministic.
9. Fast / medium / slow compute. 10 Hz is a target, not a certificate.
10. Advise before Act. Closed loop is Phase E, after shadow mode.

## Sequence

Declare → Represent → Audit → Compare → Validate → Advise → Act

If a feature skips Audit or Compare, it is a costume.
