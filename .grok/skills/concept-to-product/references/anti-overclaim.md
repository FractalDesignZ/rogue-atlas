# Anti-overclaim

Scientific products die from fluency. These are the ship-blockers.

## Never

- Call a tautological control a discovery.
- Use GUE (or any zeta null) on a drill / thought / wellbore series.
- Treat function-sheaf restriction composition as a certificate (it is an identity).
- Say PCA "found" a dimension, a time, or a spinor.
- Convert filtration scale, MD, or token index into wall-clock time.
- Mix local and recurrence edges in one graph and name it "the manifold."
- Let an LLM reading introduce facts absent from the ledger.
- Promise a Python / CUDA backend the preview does not run.
- Cite a private RMSE, Kaggle score, or unpublished kernel.
- Treat Geometric Unity, RH, or any named hypothesis as settled by a demo.
- Soften **excluded** into "suggests," "hints," or "consistent with."

## Repair, don't hedge

Wrong: "This may indicate a possible sheaf-like structure."
Right: "Sheaf gate failed. Connection only. Topology is not eligible."

Wrong: "Results approach GUE, suggesting quantum chaos."
Right: "Spacing statistic does not separate from the GUE null in this cell."

## File discipline (this sandbox)

- Never `StrReplace` a large file with empty `old_string`. You will wipe it.
- After any large edit, `wc -l` and read the top and a mid slice.
- Hydrate `localStorage` in `useEffect` or you will SSR-mismatch.
- Keep `/workspace/startup.sh` able to revive the preview.

## Voice to the user

Product terms. No ports, no containers, no "I ran tsc."
Say what they can try, and what the instrument will refuse.
