# Product vision

Rogue Atlas is an evidence-aware geometric intelligence platform.

> Transform sequential data into geometric and topological representations,
> while continuously distinguishing structure imposed by the representation
> from structure independently supported by the data.

**See the geometry. Test the claim. Deploy the evidence.**

## Three rooms

| Room | Audience | Shipped path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atlas Research | mathematicians, TDA, physics | `/studio` |
| Atlas Operations | industrial replay, first vertical = drilling | `/adapter` |
| Atlas Audit | anyone who has to sign the claim | `/atlas` |

Meridian is the scientific kernel. GT-MoE Rogue is the first vertical —
advisory only.

## Sequence

Declare → Represent → Audit → Compare → Validate → Advise → Act

Geometry proposes structure. The audit decides whether it is intrinsic,
useful, and reproducible. Only then may Operations advise.

## Positioning

**Weak:** “We use topology and Riemannian geometry to understand drilling.”

**Strong:** “We test whether geometric, topological, and AI representations
reveal validated operational structure beyond conventional baselines —
and deploy only what survives those tests.”

Enterprise name considered: Invariant Studio. Not asserted as the public
brand unless the owner chooses it.
